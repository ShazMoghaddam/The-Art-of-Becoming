# The Art of Becoming

**A personal essay on wisdom, failure, and finding your own way**

> *"Wisdom is not arrival. It is a way of moving."*

Published on **Medium** · May 2026 · 8 min read

---

I don't think of myself as a writer. But occasionally something builds up slowly enough that it has to go somewhere.

This essay is about growth — not the kind that looks good on a CV, but the quiet, unglamorous kind. The sort that happens in the spaces between things. After a loss. After a failure. After sitting in your car outside a coffee shop for twenty minutes because arranging your face into something acceptable felt like more than you had.

It's about what experience actually costs. The version of yourself you have to let go of. The slow work of showing up on ordinary Tuesdays when no one is watching. And the long, uncertain business of becoming who you actually are — rather than who you've spent years trying to appear to be.

If you've ever felt like a work in progress — this one's for you.
